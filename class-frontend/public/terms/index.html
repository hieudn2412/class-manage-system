Service overview

EDU OPS provides software for learning centers to manage classes, schedules, users, homework, documents, notifications, reporting and internal operations.

Tenant administrator responsibilities

Tenant administrators are responsible for managing users, permissions, connected Gmail accounts, center data and lawful use of the service.

Gmail notification sender

When a tenant connects Gmail, EDU OPS may send notification emails using that Gmail account. The Gmail account can be disconnected from the tenant at any time.

Acceptable use

Users must not use EDU OPS to send unlawful, misleading, abusive, spam or unauthorized email, bypass tenant isolation, access another tenant's data or interfere with service security.
